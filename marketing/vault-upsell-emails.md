# Vault offer emails

Five emails for people who already left an address. Each one sends them to the offer section on the homepage. Not published. Gemini reviews this file before Karl is pinged.

Sources: `config.js` and `funnel/index.html`. The README in this folder says not to invent statistics.

Destination for every CTA:

`https://karllange.github.io/argon-autonomous-engine/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=nurture&utm_campaign=vault_offer&utm_content=dayN#vault-offer`

Replace `dayN` with `day1` through `day5`.

What the page will show them:

- The Blueprint, $97, one time
- "The practical guide to building your own AI-driven revenue engine."
- Button: "Get the Blueprint — $97"
- Optional build kit, $297. Together, $394.
- "One-time access, sent to your email."

`config.js` states no refund, no guarantee, and no trial. These emails do not add one.

Do not send this sequence while `config.js` `mode` is `"sandbox"` and the live Payment Links are blank.

From-name in the drafts: ARGON. No personal title.
